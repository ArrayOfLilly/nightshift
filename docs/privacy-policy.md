# NightShift — Privacy Policy

## Your Privacy Matters

NightShift is a fully local app. It does not create accounts, does not sync to any cloud
service, and does not collect personal information.

---

## Data Stored on Your Mac

All app data is stored exclusively on your device using UserDefaults and local JSON files.
The following information is saved:

- AI account slot labels and reset times
- Named deadlines you create
- Snippet titles and content
- Location coordinates for sunrise/sunset (manually entered, not GPS)
- App preferences (language, font size, theme)

---

## Network Access

NightShift makes one outbound request per calendar year to sunrisesunset.io to fetch
sunrise and sunset times for your configured location. Only the coordinates you set in
Settings are sent — no device identifiers, no IP logging by this app. The result is cached
locally; no further requests are made until the next year.

---

## No Tracking or Analytics

NightShift contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no telemetry, and no advertising
framework. Your usage of this app is entirely private.
